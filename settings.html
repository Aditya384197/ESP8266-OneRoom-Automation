PHYSICAL INPUTSSwitch GPIO mapping

Choose one unique input pin per switch. “Disabled” ignores that physical switch.

All four inputs start disabled until you confirm your physical wiring. GPIO0/D3 and GPIO2/D4 must stay HIGH at reset; GPIO15/D8 must stay LOW. GPIO16/D0 requires an external pull-up resistor.

Boot safety warning: GPIO0 (D3), GPIO2 (D4) and GPIO15 (D8) are ESP8266 boot-strap pins. Their electrical level at reset matters. Do not hold switches on these pins while powering up or resetting the board. For a fail-safe four-switch installation, use an external GPIO expander or a dedicated switch interface.
